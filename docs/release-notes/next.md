**UI Consistency Audit**

A visual pass over every screen, so controls that belong together look and behave as one. Nothing you can do in Horizon has changed, and no labels or data have moved.

- Fields and buttons that sit side by side are now the same height, on every form and toolbar.
- The month switch on Month Overview no longer moves under your cursor as you click through the months, and its month picker stays on screen.
- Every button and chip shows the same gold keyboard focus ring when you move through the app with Tab.
- The columns of the History year archive and the Import history line up with their headings.
- The fields in Categories and in the Import review step look like the app's other fields.
- A dialog's panel no longer flashes a white outline when it opens, and its footer buttons stay visible.
